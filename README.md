## About

I am an Aerospace Engineering student at **Universidad Rey Juan Carlos** in Madrid, working at the intersection of **mathematical modelling, scientific computing and aerospace systems**.

My work spans numerical simulation, fluid mechanics, computational physics, embedded aerospace software, telemetry and scientific instrumentation. I am particularly interested in celestial mechanics, dynamical systems, CFD, geometric mechanics and the translation of physical models into reliable computational tools.
